Sing It Straight
Signed in with your Pollen

Sing a tiny tune. See how far off every note was.

A model writes a five to eight note tune about anything you like. You hear it, then sing it into your microphone. The pitch of your voice is measured in your browser, so each note is scored in cents (100 cents is a semitone) and is not a model's opinion. A coach model then explains those numbers, a speech model reads the advice aloud, and a transcriber checks you sang the words.
